Request deletion of NLHack Bot data

Updated 10 October 2026.

  1. Email rk625dev@gmail.com with the subject “NLHack Bot data deletion”.
  2. Identify the WhatsApp number or user identifier you used with the bot and the approximate dates of your conversations. Include only the information needed to locate the conversation.
  3. The operator will review the request, verify that it concerns your information, and respond about the information located and the action taken.

What can be deleted

The current bot keeps conversation history in server memory. That history can be cleared by restarting the service. The operator will also review any diagnostic records under their control that can be associated with your request.

Separate copies

Deleting bot-controlled information does not delete the chat on your phone or records controlled by Meta, WhatsApp, DeepSeek, Crustdata, or ngrok. Manage your WhatsApp chat through WhatsApp and contact the relevant provider about its records.
