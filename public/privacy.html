NLHack Bot privacy policy

Updated 10 October 2026. NLHack Bot is an experimental WhatsApp assistant operated by Rithesh Kumar. Contact: rk625dev@gmail.com.

Information processed

When you message the bot, it receives your message content and WhatsApp identifiers, such as your phone number or WhatsApp user identifier, profile name, message identifier, and timestamps. It uses this information to identify the conversation, understand your request, and send a response.

Services that process information

Meta and WhatsApp deliver messages. Conversation content is sent to DeepSeek to generate responses. When you request company or market research, relevant research queries and company information may be sent to Crustdata. ngrok forwards webhook traffic to the bot's server. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Conversation history and diagnostics

The current bot keeps conversation history in server memory, subject to the conversation cache's expiry, and loses that history when the server restarts. The application does not currently maintain a persistent conversation database. Operational and error logs may contain diagnostic information and are handled separately from the conversation cache. Copies in your WhatsApp chat and information retained by service providers are separate from the bot's memory.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can stop using the bot at any time or block it in WhatsApp. For access, correction, or deletion requests about information controlled by the operator, email the contact above. See the data-deletion instructions. The operator cannot directly delete copies controlled by WhatsApp, Meta, DeepSeek, Crustdata, or ngrok.

Policy updates

This page will be updated if the bot's data handling changes.
