# Brand and Positioning

_Fictional demo data. Owner: Marcus Chen, Head of Marketing; product marketing: Ira Chopra._

## Positioning statement
For product teams at B2B software companies who are drowning in unstructured customer feedback, **Lumen Insights** turns support tickets, reviews, surveys and calls into prioritised, revenue-linked product decisions - without a data team.

## Messaging pillars
1. **One source of truth for feedback** - connect every channel in a day.
2. **From themes to revenue** - see which themes affect renewals and expansion.
3. **Close the loop** - route insights to Jira and notify customers when shipped.
4. **Trusted at scale** - security and reliability for growing, regulated teams (SSO/SAML, SOC 2 Type II in progress, status page). _Pillar 4 is the Q4 focus._

## Tone of voice
Clear, calm, evidence-led. Practical over hype. Short sentences, concrete numbers. No fear-based copy, no jargon like "synergy". Humble about gaps: say "on the roadmap for Nov 2026", never imply shipped features that are not.

## Visual identity
Warm amber (#F5A524) on deep ink (#0F1B2D), generous white space, product screenshots over stock photos.

## Competitors (fictional)
| Competitor | Strength | Weakness vs Lumen |
|---|---|---|
| SignalDeck | Mature enterprise features, SAML/SCIM | Expensive, slow to set up |
| Feedbackly Pro | Strong survey tooling | Weak theme clustering |
| InsightHive | Low price | Limited integrations, no revenue linking |
| Spreadsheets + Jira | Free, familiar | Manual, no clustering |

## Proof points
- Customers report 60% less time on feedback synthesis (customer survey, n=14).
- Brightwave Health customer story (published Sep 2026).
- Benchmark Report 2026: 640 downloads in Q2.

## Claims policy
No unreleased feature claims; reference SOC 2 Type I only until the Type II report is issued. Reliability claims require Platform sign-off after the Aug 14 incident. All customer names in public material need written consent.
