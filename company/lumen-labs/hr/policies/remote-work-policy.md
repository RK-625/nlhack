# Remote Work Policy

_Fictional demo data. Effective 2026-04-01._

## Model
Lumen Labs is **hybrid-first**. The Bengaluru office (Indiranagar) is open for collaboration days; remote staff in the US (6) and EU (5) work fully remote.

- Bengaluru-based staff: 2 in-office days per week recommended (Tuesday and Thursday); managers may adjust for team rhythm.
- Fully remote roles must be agreed at offer and recorded in the HR system.
- Working from another city or country: up to 30 days per year with manager approval; longer needs Head of People + Legal sign-off (tax and permanent-establishment risk).

## Availability and overlap
- Core hours 11:00-16:00 IST; US-based teams (Sales, Marketing, one CSM) keep 3 hours of overlap with Bengaluru and are on call for US customers.
- Sync meetings are recorded; decisions are written down in the relevant doc.

## Equipment and security
- Company laptop with disk encryption, MDM and endpoint protection; no customer data on personal devices.
- Access to production and customer data only via SSO + MFA from managed devices; public Wi-Fi requires the company VPN.
- Home-office stipend INR 25,000 (India) or USD 400 (US/EU) at joining, plus a monthly internet allowance.

## Communication norms
Slack for quick questions (response within 4 working hours), email for external, docs for decisions. Escalations to P1 on-call via the pager, not DMs.

## Support roles
Support and Customer Success cover a rotating schedule so that P1 tickets get a first response within 15 minutes between 06:00 and 22:00 IST.

## Exceptions
Requests that depart from this policy go to the Head of People & Operations (Nisha Verma).
