# Leave Policy

_Fictional demo data. Effective 2026-04-01. Balances per employee are in `hr/bamboohr-export/employees.csv` (`annual_leave_balance_days`)._

## Annual (paid) leave
- India-based employees: 24 days per calendar year; US/EU employees: 25 days (plus statutory public holidays).
- Accrual: monthly, pro-rated for joiners. Joiners after July receive half the annual allotment.
- Carry-forward: up to 5 days into the next year; the rest lapses on 31 March. No cash-out except on exit (up to 10 days).
- Request via the HR system at least 5 working days ahead for 3+ days of leave; manager approval within 2 working days.
- Blackout guidance: customer-facing teams avoid overlapping leave on the same account during renewal month.

## Sick leave
- 12 days per year, no carry-forward. Medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive days.

## Parental leave
- Maternity: 26 weeks paid (India statutory), extra 4 weeks company-paid. Paternity/partner: 4 weeks paid. Adoption: same as parental.
- Can be taken flexibly within 12 months of birth/adoption.

## Other leave
- Bereavement: 5 days (immediate family), 2 days (extended).
- Marriage leave: 5 days. Comp-off: 1 day off for approved weekend/holiday on-call or incident work (expires in 60 days).
- Unpaid leave: up to 30 days with Head of People approval.
- Public holidays: 12 per year (India calendar) or local calendar for non-India staff.

## Incident time off
After a P1 incident response of more than 6 hours outside working hours, engineers get a rest day next working day without drawing leave.
