# Employee Handbook - Lumen Labs

_Fictional demo data. Version 3.2, effective 2026-04-01. Owner: Nisha Verma, Head of People & Operations._

## 1. Values
Customer truth first. Own the outcome. Write it down. Ship small, learn fast. Be kind and direct.

## 2. Employment basics
- Working hours: core hours 11:00-16:00 IST (overlap with US/EU colleagues by agreement); 40 hours/week.
- Probation: 3 months for L1-L3, 6 months for L4+. Notice period: 30 days during probation, 60 days after (90 days for L5-L6).
- Employment types: Full-time, Part-time (pro-rated benefits). Contractors are engaged under a separate agreement.
- Performance cycle: reviews each April, mid-year check-in each October. Ratings: Exceeds, Meets, Developing.
- Compensation: see `hr/salary-bands.md`. Salaries are paid on the last working day of the month.

## 3. Benefits (India)
Group health insurance for employee, spouse and two children (INR 5 lakh cover, parental top-up available); personal accident cover; PF as per statutory rules; INR 30,000 annual learning budget; meal and internet reimbursement INR 2,500/month; home-office set-up INR 25,000 on joining.
US/EU staff receive local statutory benefits plus an equivalent health stipend via a PEO/EOR.

## 4. Equity
ESOP grants by band at offer; 4-year vest, 1-year cliff, 10-year exercise window after leaving for employees with 2+ years.

## 5. Conduct
Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or retaliation. Concerns go to the Head of People (nisha.verma@lumenlabs.example) or the confidential ethics line. Conflicts of interest and outside work must be disclosed.

## 6. Confidentiality and security
Customer data is handled under least-privilege; production access requires SSO + MFA and is logged. No customer data on personal devices. Report suspected incidents to security@lumenlabs.example within 1 hour. See `legal/compliance-status.md`.

## 7. Expenses and travel
Pre-approve travel with your manager; economy flights; expense claims within 30 days via the finance tool. Client entertainment capped at $75/person.

## 8. On-call
Engineering on-call rota (weekly). On-call allowance INR 6,000/week for India staff. Currently only two engineers are on the platform rota; the Senior SRE hire (R-101) is intended to fix this.

## 9. Leaving Lumen
Exit interview, knowledge transfer plan, return of assets, final settlement within 45 days of last day.
