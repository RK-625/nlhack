# New-Hire Onboarding Checklist

_Fictional demo data. Owner: Jaya Thomas (Recruiter & People Partner) with the hiring manager._

## Before day 1 (T-7 to T-1)
- [ ] Signed offer letter, ESOP grant letter and NDA / IP assignment on file (Legal: Lena Fischer)
- [ ] Laptop ordered and enrolled in MDM; accounts created (Google Workspace, Slack, HRIS, SSO + MFA)
- [ ] Buddy assigned; first-week calendar sent; welcome email from manager and CEO
- [ ] Payroll set-up documents collected (bank, PAN/tax IDs for India; W-4/W-9 or local equivalents for US/EU)

## Week 1
- [ ] Day 1: welcome call, handbook walkthrough (`hr/policies/employee-handbook.md`), security training (phishing, data handling)
- [ ] Tool access by role: Engineering (GitHub, AWS read-only, Datadog), Sales (HubSpot), CS/Support (Zendesk, account-health sheet), Marketing (HubSpot marketing, analytics), Finance (Stripe read-only)
- [ ] Product deep-dive with Product (Vikram Shah's team): demo, ICP, pricing, roadmap including SSO
- [ ] 1:1 with manager; 30-60-90 day goals written down
- [ ] Meet CEO, CTO and Head of Customer Success

## Weeks 2-4
- [ ] Shadow 3 customer calls (CS, Sales) or 2 on-call shifts (Engineering)
- [ ] Complete SOC 2 control training and sign the acceptable-use policy
- [ ] First deliverable shipped (PR merged / first account plan / first campaign draft)
- [ ] Benefits enrolment complete

## Day 30 / 60 / 90
- [ ] Day 30: feedback check-in with manager and People partner
- [ ] Day 60: role-specific milestone review
- [ ] Day 90: probation review (L4+ at 6 months); confirm ESOP vest start

## Role add-ons
- **Senior SRE (R-101):** incident-response runbooks, paging access, post-mortem review of 2026-08-14 within week 2.
- **Customer Success Manager (R-102):** shadow Nandini Rao and Chris Doyle, inherit 8-10 accounts by day 45, learn health-score model.
- **Identity & SSO engineer (R-103):** auth architecture review with Priya Raman, security threat-model session.
