# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) - Template

_Fictional demo template, not legal advice. Owner: Lena Fischer. Version 2026.1. Use for prospects, vendors and candidates (unilateral version exists for candidates)._

**Parties:** Lumen Labs Private Limited, Bengaluru ("Lumen") and [Counterparty] ("Counterparty"). **Effective date:** [date]. **Purpose:** [evaluation of a potential business relationship / security review].

1. **Confidential Information** means non-public information disclosed in any form that is marked confidential or reasonably should be understood as confidential, including product roadmaps, security reports (SOC 2, pen-test summaries), pricing and customer data.
2. **Exclusions.** Information that is public, already known, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party without restriction.
3. **Obligations.** Use only for the Purpose; protect with at least reasonable care; disclose only to personnel and advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent obligations.
4. **Compelled disclosure.** Permitted if required by law, with prior notice where lawful.
5. **Term.** Disclosure period 2 years from the effective date; confidentiality obligations survive 5 years (trade secrets: as long as they remain trade secrets).
6. **Return/destruction** on request within 30 days, except archival copies required by law.
7. **No licence / no obligation** to enter a transaction. Roadmap items are not commitments.
8. **Security reports.** Lumen SOC 2 and pen-test documents are shared only under this NDA, watermarked and non-redistributable.
9. **Remedies.** Equitable relief available. Governing law: Karnataka, India (or the Counterparty's jurisdiction by agreement, approved by Legal).
10. **Signature.** E-signature accepted. Sales may sign unmodified templates up to Level-1 sensitivity; any redlines go to Legal.

Typical turnaround: 1 business day for unmodified, 3 business days with redlines.
