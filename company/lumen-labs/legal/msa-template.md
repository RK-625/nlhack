# Master Subscription Agreement (MSA) - Template

_Fictional demo template, not legal advice. Owner: Lena Fischer, Legal & Compliance Counsel. Version 2026.2._

Parties: Lumen Labs Private Limited ("Lumen") and [Customer legal name] ("Customer"). Effective Date: [date]. Order Forms reference this MSA.

1. **Service.** Lumen provides the Lumen Insights SaaS platform per the Order Form. Lumen may update the service provided core functionality is not materially reduced.
2. **Fees and payment.** Fees per Order Form; invoiced monthly or annually in USD; net-15 (monthly) or net-30 (annual). Late amounts accrue 1% per month. Renewal uplift capped at 7% unless otherwise agreed.
3. **Term and renewal.** Initial term per Order Form (default 12 months). **Auto-renews for successive 12-month terms** unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least [30/60/90] days before term end (standard: 30; enterprise negotiable up to 90).
4. **Service levels.** Monthly uptime target 99.5% (Enterprise 99.9%). Credits: 5% of monthly fee per 0.5-point shortfall, capped at 30%; credits are sole remedy except under clause 9.3.
5. **Customer data.** Customer owns Customer Data; Lumen processes it only to provide the service under the DPA. Data export available on request within 30 days of termination; deletion within 90 days.
6. **Security.** Lumen maintains a security programme aligned to SOC 2 and ISO 27001 controls; encryption in transit and at rest; annual penetration test; incident notification without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirmation.
7. **Confidentiality and IP.** Mutual confidentiality for 5 years after termination. Lumen retains all IP in the service; Customer grants a licence to use feedback and aggregated, de-identified data to improve the service.
8. **Warranties and disclaimers.** Service performs materially per documentation; otherwise provided "as is".
9. **Liability and termination.**
   9.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.
   9.2 Each party's aggregate liability is capped at fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim (negotiated variants: 2x for regulated customers; super-cap for data breach).
   9.3 Customer may terminate for chronic service-level failure (uptime below 99.5% in 3 of any 6 consecutive months) on 30 days' notice and receive a pro-rata refund. _Negotiated clause; present in the Northwind Logistics agreement._
   9.4 Either party may terminate for uncured material breach (30 days).
10. **Indemnity.** Lumen indemnifies against third-party IP claims; Customer against misuse claims.
11. **Governing law.** Delaware (US customers), England and Wales (UK), Karnataka (India), other as agreed; disputes via good-faith escalation, then courts of the governing jurisdiction.
12. **General.** Entire agreement; amendments in writing; assignment only with consent except in a merger.

Approval rules: any change to clauses 3, 4, 9 or governing law requires Legal approval; liability above 2x fees requires CEO approval.
